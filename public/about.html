About PriceCheck

Pricing should be a decision, not a guess.

PriceCheck was built to make project pricing more structured, explainable, and easier to approach.

Our purpose

Why PriceCheck exists

Creative professionals often know how to do the work long before they know exactly how to price it. A project can look simple until the deliverables expand, the deadline gets tighter, or the revision cycle grows.

PriceCheck exists to bring those variables into the conversation. Instead of starting with a number pulled from memory or intuition alone, you can start by looking at the project itself.

Understand first.
Calculate second.

PriceCheck separates two jobs that are easy to blur together. Gemini helps interpret the brief and extract useful information. Then a deterministic pricing engine applies the project factors to produce the estimate. This separation keeps the system easier to reason about and helps identical inputs stay consistent.

What we believe

Clarity over guesswork

A pricing decision should have a reason behind it.

Context matters

Scope, deadline, complexity, experience, client type, and revisions can all affect the work involved.

Transparency builds trust

An estimate should explain where it comes from rather than present an unexplained number.

Estimates are starting points

No pricing model can perfectly predict every client, market, or negotiation.

Technology

Built with AI, grounded in rules.

PriceCheck uses AI where language understanding is useful and deterministic logic where consistency matters. Gemini helps interpret project descriptions. The pricing engine handles the actual calculation. This combination supports flexible briefs while keeping the final calculation structured and repeatable.

An evolving product

Built to improve with real-world feedback.

PriceCheck can collect feedback about estimates, including what was ultimately charged and how the client responded. That feedback can help inform future improvements to the pricing model.

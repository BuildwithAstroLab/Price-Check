The process

How PriceCheck works

From a client brief to a structured pricing estimate, PriceCheck breaks the process into a few simple steps.

01

Start with the brief

Tell us what you’re working on.

Provide the project description, deliverables, experience level, deadline, and other relevant information. The more useful context you provide, the more useful the analysis can be.

Project description
I need a brand identity for a growing food business, including a logo system and social templates.
Professional7+ days
02

Understand the brief

PriceCheck extracts the important details.

Gemini analyzes the information you provide to identify useful project characteristics. This stage is about understanding the brief, not choosing a price.

  • Service category and project type
  • Complexity, duration, and deliverables
  • Client type and missing information
03

Calculate

The pricing engine calculates the estimate.

Once the project information has been structured, the deterministic pricing engine considers the base service price alongside experience, complexity, deadline pressure, client type, duration, deliverables, and revisions.

Base price × project modifiers = calculated quote

The result is constrained within the configured category range and rounded into a practical amount.

04

Review

You get the reasoning, not just the number.

The result includes a recommended range alongside the information used to understand the project. Review the project scope, pricing factors, explanation, missing information, and negotiation guidance before your client conversation.

05

Use the estimate

Turn the estimate into something useful.

Save an estimate in your history, download an estimate PDF, create an invoice, submit feedback after the project, or review previous estimates when you need them.

Less repeat work

Built to avoid unnecessary repeat work.

When the same request has already been completed, PriceCheck can reuse the completed result. If identical requests arrive while one is being processed, they can share that in-flight work instead of starting another calculation.

Visible progress

You can see what’s happening.

  1. Starting
  2. Extracting scope
  3. Calculating price
  4. Preparing advice
  5. Complete

An estimate, not a guarantee.

PriceCheck is a planning tool. Its estimate is based on the information you provide and the configured pricing model. Real-world pricing can vary with market conditions, location, client expectations, negotiation, positioning, and other factors. Use it as a structured starting point, not a guaranteed market rate.

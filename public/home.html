The problem

Pricing creative work shouldn’t feel like guesswork.

Creative projects rarely arrive with a perfectly defined price. Scope changes, deliverables pile up, deadlines move closer, and revisions appear. PriceCheck helps turn those moving parts into a structured estimate you can reason about before you send the quote.

The pricing model

A price is shaped by more than the deliverable.

PriceCheck’s model considers these project factors. They provide structure for an estimate; they do not guarantee a market price.

Scope

What exactly needs to be delivered?

Complexity

How involved is the work?

Deadline

How much time is available?

Experience

What level of execution are you offering?

Client

Who are you delivering the work for?

Deliverables & revisions

How much work and iteration is included?
